把鼠標移到照片上方,但不要按鼠標的鍵, 你可以看時鐘顯示上午(6:10AM),記住不要按鍵.

然後把鼠標向相片下方移, 時鐘顯示上午(7:40PM),&nbsp;日光沒有了, 天黑了。。。。。。。

&nbsp;

Now, place your mouse at the top of the photo. Don't hold button down.&nbsp;&nbsp;


You will notice it is 6:10 AM. Click mouse then release and 

bring&nbsp;the mouse down slowly over the photo.....

Night time appears and the lights come on....at 7:40 PM, it's dark!&nbsp;&nbsp;
