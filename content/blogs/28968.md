囧，原義：光明；網義：鬱悶、悲傷、無奈、無語等等，示意很好很強大，指處境困迫，喻尷尬，為難。普通話的“囧”與“窘”同音，讀起來的感覺也很容易跟窘境、窘況聯想在一起，普及速度飛快。又用其字的形象來表示“尷尬”、“無奈”、“真受不了”、“被打敗了”等意思。“囧事”的網義是指尷尬，無奈的事情。
The literal meaning of “囧” is brightness; Its representation over the internet is depression, sorrow, vain and silence, etc, signifying wonderful and powerful, in a tight situation which is embarrassing or awkward. The character “囧” is the homophone of “窘”, which makes it easy to associate with embarrassment and win its popularity. Its image is used to express “embarrassment”, “vain”, “it is intolerable”, “you are defeated”, etc. The embarrassing thing denotes that it is awkward or helpless. 

很囧？很雷人？分享你經歷或看到的“囧事”，一起來將快樂傳遞！
Is it very embarrassing or very ridiculous? Share the embarrassing thing you have experienced or seen? Let’s pass the love forward!
