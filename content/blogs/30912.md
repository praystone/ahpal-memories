You dont bring me flowers


&nbsp;


You dont bring me flowers 

你不再送花給我

You dont sing me love songs

你不再對我唱情歌

You hardly talk to me anymore

你幾乎也不再和我對話了

When you come through the door at the end of the day

一天結束之後,當你跨進那扇門

I remember when you couldnt wait to love me

我記得當時你等不及愛我

hardly wait to hold me 

等不及擁抱我

Now after loving me late at night

現在,當我們纏綿悱惻之後

its good for you and youre feeling all right

你覺得得到滿足

You just roll over and turn out the light

你就直接翻身把燈關掉

You dont bring me flowers anymore

你不再送花給我了

It used to be so natural to talk about forever

曾經是那麼的自然,當我們談到此情永久

used-to-bes dont count anymore

曾經我們以為的如此-不再是如此

They just lay on the floor till we sweep them away

它們就像躺在地上的塵埃,被我們掃除了

Baby, I remember all the things you taught me

親愛的,我記得所有你教我的事情

I learned how to laugh and I learned how to cry

我學會了如何笑和如何哭

I learned how to love and I learned how to lie

我學會了如何愛和學何說謊

So you'll think I could learn how to tell you goodbye

所以你似乎覺得我也學會如何和你說再見

You dont bring me flowers any more...

你不再買花送我了

So, you'll think I could learn how to tell you goodbye...

所以你似乎覺得我也學會如何和你說再見

You dont sing me love songs 

你不再對著我唱情歌

You dont sing you love me 

你不再唱 "你愛我"

You dont bring me flowers anymore....

你不再買花送我......
