&nbsp;

Most fortunate in my life two things, 

one is finally the time I love you exhausted.

&nbsp;One a long time ago that one day I met you.


-------我一生中最幸運的兩件事，

一件是時間終於將我對你的愛消耗殆盡。 

一件是很久很久以前有一天，我遇見你。

&nbsp;



&nbsp;

A guy will make you think he loves you，

but he really doesn't．

&nbsp;A girl will make you think she doesn't love you，

when she really does．


——— 很多時候，男人會讓你覺得他愛上了你，

其實他真沒有；

而女人會讓你覺得她不可能會愛上你，

結果她卻動了心 .
