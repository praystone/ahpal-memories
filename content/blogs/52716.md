Love In December


&nbsp;

So this is love
in the end of december
quiet night quiet stars 
and I'm here Monday to Sunday 
cause you're fragile and I'm weak 

&nbsp;

So you fall
when nights grow longer into sleep and you won't wake up 
and I'm here I'm sitting beside you and I'm awake untill the spring 

don't you worry
I'll be there for you 
don't worry about me you know me better then that 
I'll catch you if you would fall

&nbsp;

So you drift 
when the days grow colder away from me
and won't look back far away 
and I'can't guide you 
but I'm here until spring 

don't you worry
I'll be there for you 
don't worry about me 
I'll catch you if you would fall
