misunderstanding。untie。trust

&nbsp;


The life has how many misunderstanding, has not untied


If had the misunderstanding, can untie earlier, quickly unties


If you are lazy, does not think the necessity, indifferent


Then you possibly lose, not only then friend, but also has the trust


The academic society unties the misunderstanding, this is also one of you my life knowledge

&nbsp;


《誤會》。《解開》。《信任》

人生有多少的誤會，還沒有解開的

如果有了誤會，能早點解開，就快解開

如果你懶，覺得沒必要，無所謂

那你可能失去的，不只有朋友，還有信任

學會把誤會解開，這也是你我的人生學問之一
