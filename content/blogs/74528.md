Made mistakes, must engage in introspection《犯了錯，要反省》

《Made mistakes, must engage in introspection》


You do the matter, has injured others


One do the mistake, needs to learn the introspection


Will not be will only apologize with the human, but will not engage in introspection

你做的事情，傷害了別人

自己做的錯，要學會反省

不是只會跟人道歉，而不會自省
