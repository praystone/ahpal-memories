No matter has bumped into any difficult problem《不論碰到了什麼難題》

&nbsp;



&nbsp;

《No matter has bumped into any difficult problem》


Remembered looks to person discussion, but is not cannot look the person discussion.

Solves the problem, is looked that you must solve.

Could not find the human, was cannot discuss the solution issue.

&nbsp;




《不論碰到了什麼難題》


記得找對的人討論，而不是找不懂的人討論

解決問題，是看你要不要去解決

找到不懂的人，是討論不出解決的問題
