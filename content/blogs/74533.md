《What kind of sentiment, offends somebody heavily》


&nbsp;


Love, the friendship, the dear ones, offended somebody



Each kind of feelings are hurt are the pain, you offended somebody



If you have hurt others feelings, but after you, is injuried by others



Then I can only say, this is you earns, because is you offends somebody first


&nbsp;





《怎樣的感情，傷人最重》



愛情、友情、親情，傷了人


每一種感情被傷都是痛苦的，你傷了人嗎


如果你傷了別人感情，而你之後被別人傷


那我只能說，這是你應得的，因為是你先傷人的
