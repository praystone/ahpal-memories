Most of us have had a go at drawing a picture or message in the sand at the beach.

But American artist Andres Amador takes beach art to a whole other level.

Amador scopes out his sandy canvases on Google Earth before carving them out over hundreds of square metres with nothing more than a garden rake.

Then when he's done he waits for the tide to come in and wash his work away.
