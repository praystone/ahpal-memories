AGLOCO的營運不善緊急通知

✍️ cat530girl 📅 2007-12-14

作者：cat530girl

發文：2007-12-14

瀏覽：43 次 ｜ 回應：0 則

各位有加入AGLOCO上網賺錢的網友這共享網路收益的點子看來要成為歷史，也可能會成為新一波網路泡沫或創業失敗的例子。

信件主題AGLOCO Update Notice

寄件者 AGLOCO.com Service (emailservice@agloco.com)

內容

Dear XX,

We would like to update you on the status of AGLOCO’s operations. We continue to believe in the AGLOCO concept, but our revenue is currently not sufficient to give Members a meaningful distribution. And though there are increases in membership, the resulting revenue is not enough to support operating costs. As a development team we are unable to continue to use our savings to fund the operations. If any Member would like to pursue continuing the operations of AGLOCO, you may contact us at agloco1@live.com .

We would like to thank every Member for supporting our effort to bring a piece of the Internet directly to the user. We hope that we can find a way to keep the operations going.

AGLOCO Development Team

翻譯如下：

親愛XX       我們會想更新您在AGLOCO’s 操作的狀況。我們繼續相信AGLOCO 概念, 但我們的收支當前不是充足給成員意味深長的發行。並且雖然有在會員資格的增量, 收效的收支不是足夠支持營業成本。因為開發小組我們無法繼續使用我們的儲款資助操作。如果任一名成員會想追求繼續AGLOCO 的操作, 您可以與我們聯繫在agloco1@live.com 。      我們會想感謝每名成員支持我們的努力帶來網際網路的片斷直接地給用戶。我們希望, 我們能發現方式保留操作去。   AGLOCO 開發小組
