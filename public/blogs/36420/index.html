＊Wedding Day＊  

 

We both acknowledge what we came here for We take each other and walk through the open door From the first time that I saw you To the way you look tonight I was shaken by your spirit I was blinded by your light

我們都清楚為何而來 我們攜手走過那道開啟的大門 從我們第一次邂逅 到今晚看到妳 我震懾於妳出眾的氣質 妳的光芒讓我盲見其它事物

And the world that I used to see is gone without a trace Replaced by your eyes and the smile upon your face And I will not turn away ‘Cause you might disappear I was haunted by your heart And I felt that you were here

我過去的積習將不留痕跡 用妳臉上微笑的眼神來清理 我將不會離開妳 也許妳可能離去 我的心早已屬於妳 我告訴自己今晚妳將會在這裡

And funny when the time is right When lightning strikes You’re not alone Baby, I will pray for you my whole life through This day

有趣的是，當時間到來 閃電為愛而生 妳將不再孤寂 我將一生一世的為妳祈禱 就在今天

We’ll be together Husband and wife Now and forever The rest of our lives Well, take me to Heaven Take me tonight There is nothing words can say On this our wedding day

我們將在一起 成為夫妻 直到永遠 白頭偕老 帶我上天堂 就在今夜 無法用言語形容 就在我們結婚的日子裡
