Just you & me

 

by Zee Avi

 

You were sitting at the coffee table   你坐在茶几旁 where you’re reading Kierkegaard   看著Kierkegaard的書 Minutes later, you proceeded to say something that almost broke my heart 才過了一會兒　你就要說那些讓我心碎的話

You said, “Darling, I am tired of  livin’ my routined life. 你說　“親愛的　我厭倦了自己一成不變的生活 There’s so much in the world that i’d like to soak up with my eyes.”

這個世界還有好多東西我都想去見識見識” Well, baby I never did stop you from going out to explore 我說　寶貝　我不曾阻止你出發去探索這個世界 We can do it all together   (但)我們可以一起去 from the colds of the poles to the tropics of Borneo 從寒帶的南北兩極到熱帶的婆羅洲

Ba da da ba da…

Let’s pack our bags  and lie on the easy stream 讓我們收拾行囊　躺在潺潺的流水中 feel the water on our backs   感受背後水的流動 where we can carry on dreamin’   我們可以一直作白日夢 where we can finally be where we’d like to be 我們可以到任何想去的地方 Darlin’, just you and me    親愛的　只有你和我 Just you and me…   只有你和我…

So Darlin’, what do you say?    親愛的　你意下如何? Does that sound like a plan to you?   對你來說這是個好計畫嗎? We can build our own little world   我們可以打造只屬於我們的兩人世界 Where no one can come through   沒有人可以闖進來 We can live in huts made out of grass   我們可以住在用乾草搭建的小屋 We can greet father time as he walks pass   當時光老人從身旁經過　我們可以跟他打聲招呼 We can press feet into the dirt   我們可以一腳踩進土中 A little mud, no, it wouldn’t hurt   會沾到一些爛泥巴　喔　反正不會痛
