Hiding from the rain and snow

藏身於雨雪之間

Trying to forget but I won’t let go

試著忘記 但我怎能就這樣放手 rnLooking at a crowded street

看著熙熙攘攘的街道

Listening to my own heart beat

傾聽自己的心跳 rnSo many people all around the world

在這世界上這麼多的人

Tell me where do I find someone like you girl…

請告訴我在哪裏可以找到像妳一樣的女孩…

  rnTake me to your heart, take me to your soul

將我留存心間，讓我與妳靈魂相伴

Give me your hand before I’m old

在我年華老去之前 執子之手 rnShow me what love is–haven’t got a clue

Show me that wonders can be true

為我證明奇蹟能成真 rnThey say nothing lasts forever, we’re only here today

人言世事無法天長地久，我們只能此時相守

Love is now or never, bring me far away…

愛是把握現在抑或永遠不再，帶我遠走高飛…
