The sound of silence

by  Simon & Garfunkel

 

Hello darkness my old friend  暗夜 我的老友 I’ve come to talk with you again 我又來找你聊聊囉 Because a vision softly creeping 因為有一種異象緩緩的爬過 Left its seeds while I was sleeping 趁我入睡著時播下了種子

And the vision that was planted in my brain 那深植在我腦海裡的異象 Still remains with the sound of silence 仍然留存在沉默之聲裡

In restless dreams I walk alone 在無盡輾轉的夢境中我獨自行走

Narrow streets of cobble stone 在狹窄的圓石街道上 ‘Neath the hallo of a street lamp 在街燈的光暈的照耀下 I turned my collar to the cold and damp 我豎起衣領 抵御濕冷的天氣 When my eyes were stabbled by the flash of a neon light 一道耀眼的霓虹燈光刺痛我的眼睛

That split the night 光芒劃破夜空 And touched the sound of silence 也觸動了沈默之聲

And in the naked night I saw ten thousand people may be more

在炫目的燈光下 我看見成千上萬的人們 或許還不止 People talking without speaking - People hearing without listening

他們聊天而不談心 只用耳朵聽而非用心傾聽 People writing songs that voices never share 他們寫著一些沒有傳達任何心聲的歌曲

And no one dare disturb the sound of silence 而也沒有人敢去驚擾沈默之聲

“Fool” said I, “you do not know 我說"愚蠢的人啊,難道你不知道 Silence like a cancer grows 沈默會像癌細胞那樣生長蔓延 Hear my words that I might teach you 聽我對你說的有益的話 Take my arms that I might reach you” 握住我伸給你的手" But my words like silent raindrops fell 但是我的話語就像無聲落下的雨滴

And echo-ed in the wells of silence 迴響在沈默的深井裡

 

And the people bow and prayed 人們虔誠地跪拜禱告 To the neon God they made 對著自己手造的霓虹神像 And the sign flash out its warning 然而警訊已經閃現 In the words that it was forming 在字裡行間指明 And the sings said “The words of the prophers 它告訴人們"先知的箴言

Are written on the subway walls and tenement halls” 都已寫在地下鐵的牆上和廉價公寓的長廊裡" And whispered in the sounds of silence 並且在沈默之聲中低迴不已
