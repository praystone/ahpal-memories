Love In December

 

So this is love in the end of december quiet night quiet stars and I’m here Monday to Sunday cause you’re fragile and I’m weak

 

So you fall when nights grow longer into sleep and you won’t wake up and I’m here I’m sitting beside you and I’m awake untill the spring

don’t you worry I’ll be there for you don’t worry about me you know me better then that I’ll catch you if you would fall

 

So you drift when the days grow colder away from me and won’t look back far away and I’can’t guide you but I’m here until spring

don’t you worry I’ll be there for you don’t worry about me I’ll catch you if you would fall

 

 

 

 

寂靜的夜晚和星辰

 

一個星期的每一天我在這裡

 

因為你我變得脆弱

 

你迷失了,漫漫長夜你不再醒來

 

我清醒著陪伴你身邊直到春天到來

 

你不必擔心,我將陪你到任何地方

 

別為我擔心,你知道我可以做得更好

 

當你迷失時我會扶你一把

 

當時光冷逝,你離開我獨自飄泊.

 

不再回首,漸行漸遠.

 

我無法再引導你

 

但是我仍然在這裡等待春天的到來

 

你不必擔心,我將陪你到任何地方

 

別為我擔心,你知道我可以做得更好
