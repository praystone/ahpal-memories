The way we were(往日情懷)

Memories light the corners of my mind 回憶，照亮了我內心的角落 Misty water-colored memories of the way we were 那些朦朧如水彩一般、我倆過去的回憶

Scattered pictures of the smiles we left behind 散落的照片裡，有我們遺落的笑容 Smiles we gave to one another for the way we were 為我倆的過去所給予彼此的笑容

Can it be that it was all so simple then 還能像從前一樣那麼單純嗎？ Or has time rewritten every line 還是有時間重寫每一行每一句？ If we had the chance to do it all again 如果我們有機會重來一遍 Tell me, would we, could we 告訴我，可能嗎？可以嗎？

Memories may be beautiful and yet 回憶也許是美麗的 What’s too painful to remember  那痛苦得讓我們不願想起的往事是什麼？ we simply choose to forget 竟讓我們乾脆選擇了遺忘 So it’s the laughter we will remember 於是，我們將只記住歡笑 Whenever we remember the way we were 當我們回憶起我倆的過去

The way we were  我倆的過去
