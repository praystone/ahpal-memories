西城男孩 Miss You

 

 

I can’t sleep, I just can’t breathe 我無法入睡,我無法呼吸

when your shadow is all over me baby 當你的身影經過我,寶貝

don’t wanna be, a fool in your eyes 不想成為,你眼中的傻瓜

cause what we had was built on lies 因為我們擁有的一切都是基於謊言

and when our love seems to fade away 當我們的愛似乎在流逝

listen to me hear what I say 請聆聽我說的話

I don’t wanna feel the way that I do 我不想有這樣的感受

I just wanna be right here with you 我只是想在你身邊

I don’t wanna see ,see us apart 我不願意看到我們分開

I just wanna say it straight from my heart 我只是想從心底說出那話

 

I miss you.

我想你

 

what would it take, for you to see 如何能讓你看到

to make you understand that I’ll always believe 讓你明白我一直相信

you and I, can make it through

你和我,可以度過難關

and I still know, I can’t get over you 因為我仍然知道,我不能離開你

※ cause when our love seems to fade away 因為我們的愛似乎在流逝

listen to me hear what I say 請傾聽我所說的話
