Vocabulary 4000 Unit 1

✍️ Xaero 📅 2011-07-13

作者：Xaero

發文：2011-07-13

瀏覽：127 次 ｜ 回應：0 則

1 . abandon   v. 1 拋棄  ;  2 放棄                                                                                     1=desert , forsake      abandoned    adj.  3  被拋棄的                                                                                   2 = give up                                                                                                                                         3 = deserted I would never abandon my friends .  我永遠不會拋棄朋友 . An abandoned baby was found in front of my house . 我家門前發現了一個棄嬰 .

2 . able   adj.   1 有能力的                                                                                               1<->unable ,                                                                                                                                             incapable      ability     n.[C]  2  能力                                                                                                2 = capability

My husband is more able to cook than I am . 我先生比我會做菜 . Cats have the ability to see in the dark . 貓能在黑暗中看見物體.

    be able to … = be capable of …      能做 …

  1. aboard    prep. 搭乘   (火車 船 等交通工具)                    adv.  上    (車,船,飛機)

They went aboard a ship . 他們乘船 . All aboard .     請上車 (船).

go aboard = go on board    搭乘 Welcome aboard    歡迎搭乘 . 比較 abroad     在國外

  1. about    prep.    1 在…四周 ; 2 有關          adv. 3 四處  ; 4 大約                                    1 = around The boy scouts danced about the fire . 這些童子軍圍著營火跳舞 .                            2 = concerning There’s something unusual about him . 他有種不尋常的氣質.                              3 = here and there The wind scattered the leaves about .  風把樹葉吹得到處都是 .                                        4 = almost It’s about time for the train to arrive.     該是火車抵達的時候了 .

5 . above    prep.       1 在…之上     adv.   2 在上方     adj . 上述的                                 1 <-> below

Our plane was flying above the clouds. 我們的飛機在雲層上飛行 .                                2 <-> below Far above I saw an airplane going east .                                                                   3 <-> following 我看見在極高處有一架飛機往東飛去 . Please refer to the above paragraph.   請參考上一段 .

比較 over 在…上方        on    在…上面 (與基準面接觸)

6 abroad    adv.    去(在)國外                                                                                          =overseas Traveling abroad is my only hobby. 到國外旅行是我唯一的嗜好 .

  1. absence    n. [U]  1 缺席                                                                                      1 <-> presence absent   adj.   2  缺席的                                                                                             2 <-> present

Don’t speak badly of him in his absence. 不要在他背後說他的壞話. She has been absent from school for three weeks. 她已經缺三星期的課了.

in one’s absence      某人不在時 in the absence of…   由於缺乏… absent-minded         心不在焉的

In the absence of evidence , the man was set free . 由於證據不足 , 那男子被釋放了
