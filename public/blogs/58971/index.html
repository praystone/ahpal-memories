  1. absolute   adj.  1全然的                                                          1=complete , absolutely   adv.   2全然地 ;   3當然                                                     entire

That ’s absolute nonsense !  那全是胡說八道 ! It ’s absolutely impossible for me to go . 要我去絕不可能. Are you sure ? Absolutely. 你確定嗎?那當然.

9.absorb    v. 吸收 The pupils absorbed all the knowledge the teacher gave them . 學生把老師所授予的知識全吸收了. be absorbed in … 沉迷於….. He was absorbed in the book. 他沉迷於那本書 .

  1. abstract    adj. 抽象的                                                                                      <->concrete It ’s hard for me to understand any abstract concept . 我很難了解任何抽象概念.

11.   academic   adj.  1 學術的                                                                                1=scholarly         academy     n.[C]  2學院                                                                                  2=  college

In Taiwan , the academic year is from September to June . 在台灣,學年是從九月開始到隔年六月.

  1. accent   n.[U] 重音 ; 口音      v. 在…標上重音

 The accent is on the first syllable . 重音在第壹音節 She speaks English with a strong German accent . 她的英語代有很重的德國口音 .  Accent the following words on the proper syllables . 在下列單字的正確音節上標上重音.

 13. accept   v.  1 接受                                                    1<->reject , decline        acceptable   adj .   2 可接受的                                 2<-> unacceptable        acceptance   n. [U]  3 接受                                              3<-> rejection

       He could not accept that he failed the exam again.        他不能接受他考試又再度失敗 .        The decision was acceptable to all of us.        我們大家對此決定都能接受         His acceptance of the present pleased his father.         他收下那份禮物令他父親很高興

              正式                 較不正式                                   意義               accept                take                 欣然地接受 (  禮物 . 建議 . 邀請等  )               receive                get                  被動地揪受 (  被提共 / 被送來的東西 )         He received an invitation , but did not accept it.         他收到邀請函 , 但未接受邀起 .

             14. access    v.  (從電腦) 讀取 (資料)           n.[U]  1 通道 ; 2 入口              1=course , path              accessible   adj .3 易接近的                                                           2 = entrance , door        It seems to be easy for hackers to access government computer files.   3=approachable,        駭客似乎可以輕易侵入政府的電腦檔案.                                                                    handy        The island is very easy of access. 去那坐倒很容易        The place is not accessible by land . 那地方無法經由路地到達 .         easy [difficult]  of access 容易 [難以] 靠近         gain access to …    接近 …         have access to …   可進入…         within easy access of …   在易於到達…的範圍內
