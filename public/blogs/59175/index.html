15.   accident n.[C] 意外事件         accidental   adj. 1 意外的                                              1.<->intentional         accidentally   adv. 2 意外地                                      2.<->intentionally ,         My leg was broken in a traffic accident .                             on purpose         我的腿在壹次交通事故中斷了.         Three people were killed in accidental fire.          三人死於意外的火災.         I met my college classmate on the street accidentally.         我在將上巧遇我大學同學.         # by accident = by chance = accidentally    意外地

16.  accompany   v. 1陪同 ;  2 伴隨  ; 3 使附帶                 1=go with , follow        She is always accompanied by her mother.    他總是由母親陪著.   2=co-exist        Lightning usually accompanies thunder . 打雷通常伴隨閃電而來.   3=occur with        She accompanied her story with gestures. 她比手畫腳地講故事.

17.  accomplish   v. 1 完成                                                         1=carry out        accomplishment   n. [U] 2 完成 [C] 3 成果                      2 = completion        He tried to accomplish his mission.   他試圖完成任務.   3=achievement        After finishing the task , you will have a sense of accomplishment.        完成此項工作後,你會得到成就感.        The accomplishments of these scientists are amazing        這些科學家的研究成果驚人.

18.  according   adv. 根據 <to>         According to the paper , there was an earthquake in Peru.        據報載 , 祕魯發生了地震.

19.   account   n.[C] 1.說明 ; 帳戶     v. 2 解釋 <for>               1= description ,         accountant   n.[C] 會計師                                                     explanation         He gave us a detailed account of the accident .                     2=explain         他向我們詳細報告此事故.         Put the ring on my father’s account. 戒子的前算在我爸的帳上.         How do you account for your absence from the meeting ?         你如何解釋你會議缺席的原因?         #on account    作為訂金 ; 分期付款         #on one’s account   為了某人 ; 算在某人的帳上         #on account of … = because of …   因為…         #on all accounts = on every account    務必;無論如何         #on no account   絕不         #take…into account   考慮…         He paid 1,000 dollars on account . 他付一千元美金作為定金.         The picnic was put off on account of rain. 野餐因雨延期.         This must be done on all accounts. 此事務必做好.         On no account would he accept my advice. = He wouldn’t accept my accept my         advice on any account. 無論怎樣他都不聽我的勸告.         I will take his age into account. = I will take account of his age.         我會把她的年紀考慮在內.

       20. accurate   adj 1 精確的                                                              1<->inaccurate              accurately   adv. 2精確地                                                        2 = with accuracy              accuracy    n[U] 3 精確                                                            3 <->inaccuracy              My watch is very accurate. 我的表非常準確              I can’t tell you what happened accurately.              我無法精確告訴你發生了什麼事              He reported the details with accuracy. 他精確地報告事情的細節.
