A love so beautiful

The summer sun went down on our love long ago 夏天的太陽跟著墜落見證我們逝去已久的愛 But in my heart I feel the same old afterglow 但我心中感受的依然是恆久不變的餘暉 A love so beautiful in every way 不論何處那份愛如此美麗 A love so beautiful we let it slip away 而我們卻讓它溜走 We were too young to understand to ever know 我們太年輕也永遠不懂這愛的真諦 That lovers drift apart and that’s the way love goes 相知相依的愛侶因為這樣就此仳離 A love so beautiful a love so free 這愛如此瑰麗，愛一個人當是如此自由自在 A love so beautiful a love for you and me 這份愛因為妳我而如此繽紛 And when I think of you I fall in love again 當我想起妳，彷彿又墜入愛河 A love so beautiful in every way 不論何處那份愛如此美麗 A love so beautiful we let it slip away 而我們卻讓它溜走 And when I think of you I fall in love again 每當我想起妳，彷彿又墜入愛河 A love so beautiful we let it slip away 而我們卻讓它溜走 A love so beautiful in every way 不論何處那份愛如此美麗 A love so beautiful we let it slip away 但我們卻讓它溜走
