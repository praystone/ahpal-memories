劉德華 Slip away (稍縱即逝) (〒︿〒)

✍️ 註冊夠快 📅 2011-09-22

作者：註冊夠快

發文：2011-09-22

瀏覽：350 次 ｜ 回應：15 則

                                

 

Every time I look into the mirror of my mind 每當我看著自己內心世界的那面鏡子 I barely recognise ??the man I see 我幾乎不認得??……自己所看見的這個人 He used to be a dreamer, He used to be like me 他曾經是個夢想家, 曾經就像是~~,我,一樣 But I can see the sadness in his eyes 但我看見他眼裡的悲傷 And he’s no longer who I want to be 他不再是我想成為的那個人  

If I could I would change my yesterday 如果可以讓時光倒流 I would listen to my heart and then today 我會作回自己,就活在當下 I’d see What true love could be and I won’t let tomorrow 如果知道真愛是什麼,我不會讓明天~~ I won’t let tomorrow slip away 我不會這樣輕易地讓明天,稍縱即逝  

I feel the tide is turning and I’m stranded out at sea 浪又這樣打回來,我卻擱淺在海邊~~ Hoping for my rescue to arrive 希望我的拯救者到來~~ The coulds have all turned grey 所有的雲都變成灰色的~~ And now the waves are crashing in 所有的浪,洶湧而進 I thought I could survive but reality has got the better part of me 我以為我能夠生存下來,有趣的是,現實生活已經擁有了我最好的那部份

If I could I would change my yesterday 如果可以讓時光倒流 I would listen to my heart and then today 我會作回自己,就活在當下 I’d see What true love could be and I won’t let tomorrow 如果知道真愛是什麼,我不會讓明天~~ I won’t let tomorrow slip away 我不會這樣輕易地讓明天,稍縱即逝  

I’ll make a promise to myself 我會給自己一個承諾 That I will live each day just like it was the last time 我會過著每一天,就像它曾經那樣美好 I’ll ever see your face again 我將再次與你相遇  

If I could I would change my yesterday 如果可以讓時光倒流 I would listen to my heart and then today 我會作回自己,就活在當下 I’d see What true love could be and I won’t let tomorrow 如果知道真愛是什麼,我不會讓明天~~ I won’t let tomorrow slip away 我不會這樣輕易地讓明天,稍縱即逝
