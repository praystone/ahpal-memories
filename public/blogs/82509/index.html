Megaupload file-sharing site shut down                                          

Megaupload charged users a fee to upload large files anonymously

                       Megaupload, one of the internet’s largest file-sharing sites, has been shut down by officials in the US.

         The site’s founders have been charged with violating piracy laws.

         Federal prosecutors have accused it of costing copyright rnholders more than $500m (£320m) in lost revenue. The firm says it was rndiligent in responding to complaints about pirated material.

         The news came a day after anti-piracy law protests, but investigators said they were ordered two weeks ago.

         The US Justice Department said that Megaupload’s two rnco-founders Kim Dotcom, formerly known as Kim Schmitz, and Mathias rnOrtmann were arrested in Auckland, New Zealand along with two other rnemployees of the business at the request of US officials. It added that rnthree other defendants were still at large.

         “This action is among the largest criminal copyright cases everrn brought by the United States and directly targets the misuse of a rnpublic content storage and distribution site to commit and facilitate rnintellectual property crime,” said a statement posted on its website.

  Third-party sites       The charges included copyright infringement, rnconspiracies to commit racketeering, copyright infringement and money rnlaundering.

         A federal court in Virginia ordered that 18 domain names associated with the Hong Kong-based firm be seized.

         The Justice Department said that more than 20 search warrants rnhad been executed in nine countries, and that approximately $50m in rnassets had been seized.

         It claimed that the accused had pursued a business model designed to promote the uploading of copyrighted works.

         “The conspirators allegedly paid users whom they specifically rnknew uploaded infringing content and publicised their links to users rnthroughout the world,” a statement said.

         “By actively supporting the use of third-party linking sites torn publicise infringing content, the conspirators did not need to rnpublicise such content on the Megaupload site.

         Before it was shut down the site posted a statement saying the allegations against it were “grotesquely overblown”.

美國聯邦政府周四發動大規模打擊盜版行動，強勢關閉檔案分享網站 Megaupload.com，並逮捕多位負責人。

不過 Megaupload 支持者隨即反撲，駭客集團「Anonymous」將苗頭指向美國司法部，宣稱已癱瘓其網站。

美國司法部發言人向《CNNMoney》證實，司法部網站出問題，但原因不明。與「Anonymous」有關的推特帳戶，事先發出攻擊預告。

Anonymous 擅長透過所謂的「分散式阻斷服務」(DDoS ) 來攻擊，用流量來灌爆網站。但該團體從未傳出涉及竊取資料或危安。

推特帳號 AnonOps 同時也預告癱瘓 universalmusic (環球音樂) 網站，台北時間 8：30 左右，該網站仍連不上。

環球音樂正在和 Megaupload 打官司，兩件事可能有關聯。RIAA (美國唱片業協會) 及 MPAA (美國電影協會)網站也癱瘓，AnonOps 也宣稱是他們的傑作。

周四稍早，美國司法部和聯邦調查局以「非法運作一全球性盜版組織」的名義，起訴與 Megaupload 相關 7 人，網站創辦人 Kim Dotcom (原名Kim Schmitz) 等 4 人在紐西蘭被捕，其他 3 人在逃。

聯邦調查局說，經營 Megaupload 的非法獲利超過 1.75 億美元，大多來自廣告費和優質會員費。

根據起訴狀，2005 年創立的 Megaupload，非法散布受版權保護的電視劇、圖片、電腦軟體和電玩遊戲。

被起訴的 7 人都不是美國公民，而是紐西蘭、德國、斯洛伐克與荷蘭人。不過 Megaupload 有伺服器設在美國華盛頓特區和維吉尼亞州。
