Archer無限劍制

✍️ 月楓燁 📅 2012-02-02

作者：月楓燁

發文：2012-02-02

瀏覽：0 次 ｜ 回應：0 則

I am the bone of my sword. 此身之劍所制 Steel is my body and fire is my blood. 血為鋼鑄 心似琉璃 I have created over a thousand blades. 歷經無數戰場而不敗 Unknown to Death. 不曾遭逢敗退 Nor known to Life. 不曾為人所理解 Have withstlld pain to create many weapons. 其常獨自於劍塚之巅陶醉於勝利之中 Yet,those hands will never hold anything. 因此,此生無須任何意義 So as I pray,unlimited blade works. 則其身,無限之劍制
